# Karnataka MGNREGA Work Demand Analysis (FY 2024–25)

## Project overview
This project analyses district-wise monthly household demand for work under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) in Karnataka during FY 2024–25.

## Business questions
1. How did recorded household demand vary month by month across Karnataka?
2. Which districts recorded the highest and lowest cumulative monthly demand?
3. How did demand change compared with the previous month?

## Dataset
- Geography: Karnataka
- Period: Financial year 2024–25 (April–March)
- Coverage: 31 districts and 12 months
- Granularity: District-month
- Measure: Households demanding work

## Tools and skills
- **Python:** Pandas, Matplotlib
- **SQL:** Google BigQuery

## Key findings
- May had the highest monthly demand: **1,528,799**.
- October had the lowest monthly demand: **568,443**.
- Belagavi had the highest cumulative district total: **1,099,019**.
- Bengaluru had the lowest cumulative district total: **5,491**.
- The largest month-on-month decline was in August: **−35.38%**.
- The largest month-on-month increase was in March: **+48.58%**.
